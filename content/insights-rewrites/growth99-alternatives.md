# Growth99 Alternatives for Med Spas: 2026 Comparison

**Short answer:** Growth99 is a software platform plus marketing team built for aesthetic and wellness practices, and its prices aren't published. If you own one or two med spa locations in Iowa or the Midwest, compare it with Etna Interactive, DoctorLogic, Intrepy and a local agency on the same questions: price, terms, after-hours replies and what you keep.

This guide sticks to what each company publishes on its own site.

Where something isn't public, we say so.

All sites were checked in October 2026.

## What is Growth99?

Growth99 calls itself a [full-stack growth platform built for aesthetic and elective wellness practices](https://growth99.com/).

It handles websites, SEO, paid media, marketing automation and AI-powered lead response.

Its industry list includes med spas, wellness, plastic surgery, dental, dermatology, laser hair removal, salons and IV therapy.

Its [pricing page](https://growth99.com/pricing-plans/) shows three plan tiers. The plans list:

- An AI chatbot trained on aesthetic terms
- 24/7 SMS and email automation
- A review generator
- A multi-location add-on

Paid media and social media are add-ons. Those say "Pricing available upon request."

The paid media tiers manage monthly ad budgets of up to $1,500, $3,500 and $8,000 on Google and Meta.

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

## Which agencies do med spas compare with Growth99?

### Etna Interactive

Etna calls itself a [full-service medical marketing agency](https://www.etnainteractive.com/).

It serves plastic surgery, facial plastic surgery, med spas, dermatology and ophthalmology.

It lists call tracking, lead management and reputation products.

Its [med spa page](https://www.etnainteractive.com/clients/medical-spa-marketing/) covers SEO, paid ads, reputation, web design, social, email and staff training.

It gives a San Luis Obispo, CA address and says it is fully remote, serving clients nationwide.

It says it has specialized in aesthetics for more than 20 years.

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

### DoctorLogic

DoctorLogic says it works only in healthcare.

Its [med spa page](https://doctorlogic.com/specialties/medical-spa-marketing) lists website design, SEO, content, Google Ads, reputation management, SmartAI tools and before-and-after galleries.

The next step it offers is a 20-minute demo.

Its [pricing page](https://doctorlogic.com/pricing) shows no prices, setup fees or contract terms.

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

### Intrepy

Intrepy [markets to private medical practices](https://intrepy.com/) across 40+ subspecialties, including med spas and multi-location groups backed by private equity.

Its [med spa page](https://intrepy.com/medical-marketing/medical-spa-marketing/) lists SEO, ads on Google, Meta, YouTube and programmatic, social media, websites, reputation management and growth dashboards.

It also offers CaptureMD, its own video tool.

It says it has spent nearly 10 years marketing medical spas.

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

## When might Growth99 or another agency be the better fit?

Be honest with yourself about size. These are good reasons to pick one of them:

- **You run several locations.** Growth99 sells a multi-location add-on. Intrepy works with multi-location groups.
- **You want software and marketing from one aesthetics vendor.** Growth99 is built around its own platform.
- **You also run a plastic surgery or dermatology practice.** Etna serves those specialties next to med spas.
- **You want a healthcare-only team with before-and-after galleries built in.** DoctorLogic lists both.
- **You want a national team and don't need to meet in person.** Etna, for one, says it is fully remote and serves clients nationwide.

If none of those describes you, keep reading.

## What should you ask every med spa marketing agency?

Use the same list on all of them, including us:

- What is the monthly price, in writing?
- Is there a setup fee, and how long is the agreement?
- Do you take a cut of ad spend?
- What happens when someone calls during a treatment, or messages at 9 PM?
- When does the website become mine?
- What do you commit to, and how do I leave if you miss it?
