# Med Spa SEO in 2026: What It Costs and What Actually Works

**Short answer:** med spa SEO in 2026 typically costs a single-location spa about $1,000 to $3,000 monthly, depending on how crowded the market is, and multi-location groups $2,500 to $6,500 or more. Treatment searches often move in the first three months; Google Maps gains take three to six.

| Med spa type | Typical monthly cost | Typical one-time onboarding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single location, low competition | $1,000 to $1,500 | $500 to $1,500 |
| Single location, competitive metro | $1,500 to $3,000 | $500 to $1,500 |
| Multi-location group | $2,500 to $6,500+ | Varies by scope |

Ranges are from [The Search Sherpa's 2026 med spa SEO pricing guide](https://thesearchsherpa.com/med-spa-seo-cost/). For context across all industries, [Ahrefs' survey of 439 SEO providers](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/) found $501 to $1,000 was the most common monthly retainer.

## Why is med spa SEO getting harder?

There are more spas competing for the same searches. The [American Med Spa Association's 2024 industry report](https://www.americanmedspa.org/blog/2024-medical-spa-state-of-the-industry-executive-report-recap) counted 10,488 med spa locations in 2023, up from 8,899 a year earlier. 81 percent are single-location businesses, so most are competing on local search without a big brand behind them.

The same report found 73 percent of med spa patients are repeat patients. SEO is how the other 27 percent find you in the first place.

## What actually works in med spa SEO?

People don't search "med spa". They search the treatment: "Botox West Des Moines", "lip filler near me", "laser hair removal". Med spa SEO comes down to showing up for those searches.

What moves the needle:

1. **A page per treatment.** Named the way people search, with who performs it, what to expect and how to book.
2. **A complete Google Business Profile.** [Backlinko's local SEO statistics](https://backlinko.com/local-seo-stats) report that people are 2.7 times more likely to see a business as reputable when its profile is complete, and 42 percent of local searchers click in the Maps pack.
3. **Provider credentials in plain view.** Who injects, and their training, on the page and on the profile.
4. **Fresh photos.** Before-and-after images only with signed client consent.
5. **Steady, recent reviews.** [BrightLocal's 2025 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey-2025/) found just 4 percent of consumers never read online reviews.

What usually wastes money:

- Generic blog posts that don't match any treatment people search for
- Ranking reports with no calls or bookings attached
- Review requests that mention the client's treatment, or offer something in exchange

## How long does med spa SEO take?

The Search Sherpa guide says local and treatment terms usually show movement inside the first three months, and meaningful Map Pack gains land in three to six months. Dense metros can take nine to twelve.

A fair plan looks like this:

- **First month:** Google profile cleaned up, treatment pages live, review requests running
- **Months 2 to 3:** first movement on treatment and neighborhood searches
- **Months 3 to 6:** steadier map rankings, more calls and booking requests
- **After that:** new treatments and seasonal promotions get their own pages

## What is medical spa digital marketing beyond SEO?

SEO is one piece. The full picture for most spas is:

- **Search and Maps (SEO):** steady treatment searches, slower to build, keeps working
- **Paid ads:** fast for launches and promotions, stops when the budget stops
- **Social media:** keeps current clients engaged, rarely the first touch for a new one
- **Fast replies:** a booking request answered in an hour often goes to whoever answered in a minute

That last one is where spas lose the most. A client who messages at 9 PM about filler and hears nothing until morning has often booked somewhere else.

## How do you choose a med spa SEO company?

Ask the same questions of every agency:

- **What do you do each month, line by line?**
- **How will I see calls and bookings from Google?**
- **Who owns the website and the Google profile?**
- **How do you handle before-and-after photos and review requests?**
- **What are you promising?** Anyone promising a ranking is promising something nobody controls.
