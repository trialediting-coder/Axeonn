# Scorpion Alternatives for Practices and Firms (2026)

**Short answer:** Scorpion is a large national marketing company built for law firms, health systems and multi-location brands. If you run one dental practice, med spa, law firm or CPA firm in Iowa or the Midwest, compare it with a national vertical agency, a local agency, a freelancer and Axeon on the same questions: price, terms, ownership and what happens to every call.

This guide sticks to what each company publishes. Where something isn't public, we say so.

## What is Scorpion?

Scorpion is a national marketing and technology company. [Scorpion's site](https://www.scorpion.co/) lists Lehi, Utah as its location.

It lists these industries:

- Legal
- Medical, and hospital and healthcare systems
- Franchise and multi-location brands
- Home services

For law firms, [Scorpion's legal page](https://www.scorpion.co/legal/) lists personal injury, criminal defense, family law, immigration, and estate and probate.

It also describes Scorpion as Clio's sole preferred marketing provider and Google's largest Local Services Ads partner.

For healthcare, [Scorpion's healthcare page](https://www.scorpion.co/healthcare/) describes websites "purpose-built for health systems with multiple locations."

It also has a [page for dentists](https://www.scorpion.co/healthcare/dentists).

We didn't find a med spa or accounting page on Scorpion's site (checked October 2026).

## What does Scorpion include?

From Scorpion's own pages:

- Websites, SEO, paid ads and reputation tools
- **Scorpion Connect** with AI chat that responds, books appointments and answers questions, per its [dentist page](https://www.scorpion.co/healthcare/dentists)
- Named tools such as Ranking AI and Reputation AI
- One dashboard for marketing, intake and revenue, per its [legal page](https://www.scorpion.co/legal/)
- A typical website launch of four to 10 weeks

## How much does Scorpion cost?

Quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

The [healthcare page](https://www.scorpion.co/healthcare/) says the investment "depends on your business goals."

Ask for a written quote that splits setup, monthly fees and ad spend.

## What are Scorpion's contract and ownership terms?

Scorpion's FAQ on its [healthcare page](https://www.scorpion.co/healthcare/) says:

- It "typically requires a 12-month contract" for its marketing technology.
- Digital advertising is "typically month-to-month."
- You own your website after the contract is complete.
- You don't own the underlying CMS. Domains, content and imagery are yours.

That is a clear answer. Ask how the site moves if you leave, since the CMS stays with Scorpion.

## What do reviews say about Scorpion?

Reviews are mixed. On [Clutch](https://clutch.co/profile/scorpion), some reviewers praise responsiveness and legal marketing knowledge, and some mention concerns about cost and contract terms.

## What are the real alternatives to Scorpion?

A small practice or firm usually weighs five options.

### 1. Scorpion

A big national vendor with its own tech platform. Strong fit for multi-location brands and large ad budgets.

### 2. Other national vertical agencies

These focus on one profession. Two common ones:

- **FindLaw** (law firms). [FindLaw's marketing page](https://www.findlaw.com/lawyer-marketing/) calls it a "one-stop law firm marketing provider." It lists websites, local SEO, directory ads, PPC, call answering, web chat and an INSIGHT dashboard. No pricing is published.
- **ProSites** (dental). [ProSites](https://www.prosites.com/) lists dental websites, SEO, Google Ads, an AI receptionist, AI chat, online booking and a performance dashboard. Its [pricing page](https://www.prosites.com/pricing/) names plans but shows no dollar amounts for them.

### 3. A local agency

Someone in your metro you can meet. Quality varies a lot.

Ask whether they handle after-hours calls and booking, or only the website and SEO.

### 4. A freelancer

Often the lowest cost for a website.

Most don't run phones, texting, booking or reporting. You become the project manager.

### 5. Axeon

A West Des Moines team built for single-location practices and firms. Details below.

## When may Scorpion be the better fit?

Be honest about your size. Scorpion may suit you better if:

- You run a multi-location group or DSO
- You're a franchise brand
- You have a national or multi-state ad budget
- You want one large vendor for everything across many offices
- You're a health system with many service lines

That's who its healthcare pages are written for.

## What should each kind of office ask?

### Dental practices

Ask who answers when the front desk is busy with a patient. Ask what SEO costs on its own. See [how much dental SEO costs](/insights/how-much-does-dental-seo-cost) and our [dental page](/solutions/dental).

### Med spas

Ask whether the vendor has a med spa offer, not just a general medical one. Ask how consult requests get booked after hours. See [med spa](/solutions/med-spa).

### Law firms

Ask what the intake form collects. It shouldn't ask for case details. Ask who owns the site after the term. See [what a law firm website costs](/insights/how-much-does-a-law-firm-website-cost) and [law firms](/solutions/law-firms).

### CPA and accounting firms

Ask how the system handles call volume in tax season. Ask whether the vendor serves accounting firms at all. See [accounting](/solutions/accounting).

## How do you choose?

Put every option through the same questions:

- What's the full monthly cost, in writing?
- What's the term, and how do I get out?
- Who owns the site, the domain and the data?
- What happens to a call at 9 p.m.?
- Is ad spend marked up?
