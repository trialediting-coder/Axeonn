# 14 Med Spa Marketing Ideas That Respect Your Clients

**Short answer:** the med spa marketing ideas that work are a page per treatment, injector credentials in plain view, before-and-after photos only with signed consent, steady clean reviews, and a fast reply to every call and message. Here are 14, grouped so you can start with a few.

| Group | Ideas | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get found | 1 to 3 | Clients search the treatment, not "med spa" |
| Earn trust | 4 to 8 | Credentials, consent and reviews decide who gets the call |
| Make booking easy | 9 to 11 | A slow reply often means a lost consultation |
| Keep clients | 12 to 14 | Most of your business is repeat clients |

There are more spas competing every year. The [American Med Spa Association's 2024 report](https://www.americanmedspa.org/blog/2024-medical-spa-state-of-the-industry-executive-report-recap) counted 10,488 US locations in 2023, up from 8,899 a year earlier, and 81 percent are single-location.

## Which med spa marketing ideas help clients find you?

**1. Build a page for each treatment.**
Neurotoxins, filler, laser hair removal, microneedling, chemical peels. Named the way people search, with what to expect, who performs it and how to book. No promises about results.

**2. Finish your Google Business Profile.**
Treatments, hours, photos, booking link. [Backlinko's roundup of Google data](https://backlinko.com/local-seo-stats) says people are 2.7 times more likely to see a business as reputable when its profile is complete, and 42 percent of local searchers click in the Maps pack.

**3. Add pages for nearby towns.**
Written for real clients in each area, not copies with the name swapped.

## How do med spas earn trust online?

**4. Show injector credentials.**
Who performs each treatment, their license and their training, on the treatment page and the team page. Clients want to know who is holding the needle.

**5. Use before-and-after photos only with signed consent.**
This is the rule, not a tip:

- Signed consent before the photo is taken
- The consent says where the photo will appear (site, Google, social)
- Same lighting and angle, no filters or editing
- Removed promptly if the client asks
- No photo without consent, ever

**6. Ask every client for a review.**
A direct link by email or text after each visit. [BrightLocal's 2025 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey-2025/) found just 4 percent of consumers never read online reviews.

**7. Keep reviews clean.**
The [FTC's rule on fake reviews](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials) bans fake reviews, including AI-written ones, and any incentive tied to a positive or negative review. No "free add-on for 5 stars". Never mention the client's treatment in the request or your reply.

**8. Post short videos from your own team.**
A walk through the space, an injector explaining a consultation. BrightLocal found over three-quarters of consumers watch video when researching local businesses, and more than a third prefer videos posted by the business itself.

## How do you make it easier to book a med spa consultation?

**9. Offer online booking for consultations.**
In a [Kyruus survey of 1,000 healthcare consumers](https://www.techtarget.com/patientengagement/news/366584384/Digital-Patient-Experience-Hinges-on-Online-Appointment-Scheduling), 61 percent said online scheduling was extremely or very important.

**10. Text back every missed call.**
Your front desk and injectors are often with clients. A text from your number keeps the caller from moving on. See how [missed call text back](/insights/missed-call-text-back) works.

**11. Reply to messages at night.**
A client asking about filler at 9 PM who hears nothing until morning has often booked somewhere else. See [med spa lead generation](/marketing-solutions/lead-generation/med-spa).

## How do you keep med spa clients coming back?

The AmSpa report found 73 percent of med spa patients are repeat patients. Retention is most of the business.

**12. Send treatment-interval reminders.**
With consent, a simple "you may be due for your next appointment" text. Nothing about the client's health or treatment details in the message.

**13. Give seasonal promotions their own page.**
A promotion that sends people to the homepage loses them. One page, one offer, one booking button.

**14. Make referrals easy.**
A "bring a friend" card or email. Check your state's rules before offering anything in return.

## What med spa marketing wastes money?

- Ads that land on the homepage instead of a treatment page
- Before-and-after galleries without signed consent on file
- Social posts with no booking link
- Influencer posts with no disclosure
- Any campaign running while calls and messages go unanswered

## Which ideas should you start with?

If you only do three this month:

1. Treatment pages with injector credentials (ideas 1 and 4)
2. Clean review requests after every visit (ideas 6 and 7)
3. Text back every missed call (idea 10)
