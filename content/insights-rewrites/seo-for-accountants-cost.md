# SEO for Accountants: What It Costs and How Long It Takes

**Short answer:** SEO for accountants costs most CPA and accounting firms about $500 to $2,500 monthly in 2026, and ranking new pages for competitive local searches takes about three to six months. Start in the fall so pages are settled before tax season.

| Who does the work | Typical cost | Good fit for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freelancer | About $1,150 monthly on average | A solo CPA with one office and simple services |
| Local agency | About $1,819 monthly on average | A firm that wants profile, pages and reporting handled |
| Hourly consultant | About $111 an hour on average | A one-time audit or a fix list your staff carries out |
| Typical accounting firm budget | $500 to $2,500 monthly | Most small and mid-size firms |

Averages are from [Ahrefs' SEO pricing survey, as summarized by OuterBox](https://outerboxdesign.com/articles/seo/seo-pricing-costs) and [Ahrefs' survey of 439 SEO providers](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/). The accounting-firm range is from [a CPA SEO provider's pricing guide](https://alwaysdobetterllc.com/cpa-seo/).

## How much does SEO cost for accountants?

The price follows three things: how many firms compete in your town, how many services you want found for, and how much of the writing you want done for you.

Across all industries, Ahrefs found $501 to $1,000 was the most common monthly retainer, and 68.8 percent of providers charge $2,000 or less. A local CPA firm usually sits in that band. A multi-office firm competing in a metro sits above it.

What you should not pay for:

- Ranking reports with no calls or consultations attached
- Generic "tax tips" articles in place of pages for the services you actually want more of
- A long minimum term with nothing listed for each month

## Why does local search matter for a CPA firm?

Business owners look for an accountant close by. [Backlinko's local SEO statistics](https://backlinko.com/local-seo-stats) report that 46 percent of Google searches have local intent, and 76 percent of people who search "near me" visit a business within a day.

The searches are specific: "small business CPA Ankeny", "bookkeeping West Des Moines", "tax preparation near me". A firm with one general services page competes for none of them well.

## How long does SEO take for accountants?

Plan on a three- to six-month build for competitive local searches. Google Business Profile fixes and review requests can lift calls sooner, often within weeks.

New pages take time to earn trust. [Ahrefs' study of how long pages take to rank](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank/) found only 1.74 percent of newly published pages reach the top 10 within a year, and 72.9 percent of top-10 pages are more than three years old. Local searches are far less crowded than national ones, but the lesson holds: start early and keep going.

That makes the calendar matter:

- **September to October:** profile cleanup, service pages built, review requests running
- **November to December:** pages settle, first movement on local searches
- **January to April:** business owners search for a CPA and find you
- **After tax season:** advisory and bookkeeping pages carry the rest of the year

## What should a CPA SEO retainer include?

A fair monthly retainer for an accounting firm covers:

1. **Google Business Profile upkeep.** Services, tax-season hours and photos kept current.
2. **Separate service pages.** Tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll and advisory each get their own page.
3. **Pages for the towns your clients are in.** Written for real clients, not swapped town names.
4. **Review requests after finished engagements.** Timed to finished returns, with nothing about the client's finances.
5. **Call tracking and a monthly report.** Calls and booked consultations from Google, not just traffic.

Pages explain your services and who they're for. Anything that reads as tax advice is yours to write or approve.
