# 15 Dental Marketing Ideas That Bring In New Patients

**Short answer:** the dental marketing ideas that work are the ones every new patient touches: a complete Google profile, a page per procedure, steady reviews, easy online booking and a fast reply to every call. Here are 15, grouped so you can pick a few and start this month.

| Group | Ideas | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get found | 1 to 4 | Patients search Google before they call |
| Earn trust | 5 to 8 | Reviews and real photos decide who gets the call |
| Make booking easy | 9 to 12 | Missed calls and slow replies lose patients |
| Keep patients | 13 to 15 | Recall and referrals cost less than new ads |

## Which dental marketing ideas help patients find you?

**1. Finish your Google Business Profile.**
Hours, services, photos, booking link, insurance notes. [Backlinko's roundup of Google data](https://backlinko.com/local-seo-stats) says people are 2.7 times more likely to see a business as reputable when its profile is complete.

**2. Build a page for each procedure.**
Implants, clear aligners, crowns, emergency visits, children's dentistry. One page each, named the way patients search.

**3. Add pages for the towns you serve.**
Write for real patients in each town, not the same page with the name swapped.

**4. Make a real new-patient page.**
What the first visit includes, insurance you take, payment options, and a booking button at the top.

## How do dental practices earn trust online?

**5. Ask every patient for a review.**
In a [Dentaly survey of 1,099 Americans](https://www.dentaly.org/us/research/dentist-reviews-online/), 8 in 10 said reviews are their first step in choosing a new dentist, and 52 percent would only consider a dentist with at least 10 reviews. [BrightLocal's 2025 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey-2025/) found email was the request people were most likely to answer, at 40 percent.

**6. Keep review requests clean.**
No discounts or gifts tied to a good review. The [FTC's rule on fake reviews](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials) bans incentives conditioned on a positive or negative review. Never mention the patient's treatment in the request.

**7. Reply to reviews, good and bad.**
Short, kind, and never confirming that the reviewer is a patient or what they had done. The Dentaly survey found 95 percent say negative reviews make them think twice.

**8. Use real photos and short videos.**
Your team, your rooms, your front door. BrightLocal found over three-quarters of consumers watch video when researching local businesses.

## How do you make it easier to book a dental appointment?

**9. Offer online booking.**
At least for new-patient exams and cleanings. In a [Kyruus survey of 1,000 healthcare consumers](https://www.techtarget.com/patientengagement/news/366584384/Digital-Patient-Experience-Hinges-on-Online-Appointment-Scheduling), 61 percent said online scheduling was extremely or very important to them.

**10. Text back every missed call.**
In one [analysis of 4,280 calls across a 26-practice dental group](https://www.peerlogic.com/post/peerlogic-dso-case-study), 38 percent went unanswered. That data comes from a call-answering vendor, but the lesson holds. See how [missed call text back](/insights/missed-call-text-back) works.

**11. Answer web inquiries in minutes, not hours.**
A patient who fills out a form at lunch has often booked elsewhere by dinner.

**12. Cover after-hours.**
A chat or text reply at night that offers booking times. See [AI chat and scheduling for dental](/marketing-solutions/ai-chat-scheduling/dental).

## How do you keep the patients you already have?

**13. Run recall reminders.**
Text or email patients who are overdue for a cleaning, with consent and nothing about their health in the message.

**14. Make referrals easy.**
A simple "we'd love to see your family too" card or email. Check your state dental board's rules before offering anything in return.

**15. Show up locally.**
School talks, youth sports sponsorships, community events. Post the photos on your profile.

## What dental marketing ideas waste money?

- Paying for ads while calls go to voicemail
- Social posts with no booking link
- Generic blog posts that match no procedure people search for
- Ranking reports with no calls or booked exams attached

## Which ideas should you start with?

If you only do three this month:

1. Finish the Google profile (idea 1)
2. Start asking for reviews by email after every visit (ideas 5 and 6)
3. Text back every missed call (idea 10)

Then build the procedure pages and online booking. For where new patients slip away, see [how to get more dental patients](/insights/how-to-get-more-dental-patients).
