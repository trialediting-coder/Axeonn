# CPA Site Solutions Alternatives for Small CPA Firms (2026)

**Short answer:** CPA Site Solutions and Build Your Firm are low-cost template-site options with portals and newsletters. TaxProMarketer and Hinge are full marketing agencies. If you run a 1 to 15 person CPA, tax or bookkeeping firm in Iowa or the Midwest and want a site plus fast replies, booking, reviews and tracking from a local team, Axeon is the closer fit.

This guide sticks to what each company publishes. Where something isn't public, we say so.

## Who are the main CPA marketing companies?

These four come up most when firm owners search for a CPA marketing company or a CPA Site Solutions alternative:

- **CPA Site Solutions:** websites, portals and newsletters for accountants
- **Build Your Firm:** websites and marketing for CPAs, EAs and bookkeepers
- **TaxProMarketer:** a marketing agency for accountants and tax pros
- **Hinge:** a branding and marketing firm for professional services

We skipped practice software like Karbon. It runs your firm, not your marketing.

## What does CPA Site Solutions offer?

[CPA Site Solutions' homepage](https://www.cpasitesolutions.com/) calls it "The Largest Provider of Websites for Accountants" and says 10,000+ firms use it.

It lists:

- 150+ ready-to-go designs, or custom options
- SEO, pay-per-click and social media management
- A secure client portal and a client newsletter
- OfficeMojo automated appointment reminders

**Price, as listed on their site, October 2026** ([pricing page](https://www.cpasitesolutions.com/pricing)):

- Silver: $90.00/mo
- Gold: $111.00/mo
- Platinum: $137.00/mo
- Diamond: $166.50/mo

Every plan includes a secure portal and a monthly client newsletter. Call tracking and recording is in Diamond.

SEO and pay-per-click are "Call for Quote." Social media is "as low as" $79 monthly.

The page says "No hidden fees" and that you can cancel at any time. It offers a 60-day free trial.

## What does Build Your Firm offer?

[Build Your Firm](https://www.buildyourfirm.com/) builds sites for CPAs, Enrolled Agents and bookkeepers.

It lists:

- Free SEO "with every hosting level"
- Google and Facebook ads
- Content Marketing in a Box (reviews, social and blog posts)
- An Outsourced Marketing Program
- A newsletter email tool and secure file sharing

**Price, as listed on their site, October 2026** ([pricing page](https://www.buildyourfirm.com/accounting-website-marketing-pricing)):

- Professional: starting at $95.00/mo, 30-day free trial
- Platinum: starting at $120.00/mo, 60-day free trial
- Niche: starting at $120.00/mo, plus a one-time setup fee that "varies by niche"
- Do It For Me + Platinum: a higher tier, 90-day free trial

Online advertising is "Request Pricing."

Its [ROI money-back page](https://www.buildyourfirm.com/websites/roi-money-back-guarantee) says it refunds the Content Marketing in a Box investment if the website doesn't bring in more new business than it costs during the first 12-month paid subscription.

## What does TaxProMarketer offer?

[TaxProMarketer](https://www.taxpromarketer.com/) is a marketing agency for accountants and tax professionals.

It lists ads, SEO, local listings, websites, email, social media and a marketing CRM. It offers a free strategy session.

**Price, as listed on their site, October 2026** ([pricing page](https://taxpromarketer.com/pricing/)):

- Startup Package: $397 monthly
- Monthly programs: $597 to $10K monthly
- Website setup: a one-time $997 fee for programs that include a website

Ad spend is on top. The page doesn't state contract terms.

## What does Hinge offer?

It lists research, positioning, branding, website redesign and thought leadership.

Price: quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

## What is the tradeoff with a template site?

A template site is cheap and fast. You get a clean page, a portal and newsletter content.

What it usually doesn't do:

- Text back a missed call at 7 p.m. in March
- Reply to a web inquiry in under a minute
- Ask every finished client for a review
- Show you which calls came from Google

For a firm that's buried in returns from January to April, those gaps are where new clients go to the next CPA. Our guide to [missed-call text-back](/insights/missed-call-text-back) covers why.

## When may CPA Site Solutions or Build Your Firm be the better fit?

Be honest about what you need. A template provider may suit you better if:

- Referrals already fill your calendar
- You mainly want a low-cost site, a portal and a newsletter
- You want to try before you pay, with a free trial
- You'd rather handle calls and reviews in-house

TaxProMarketer may fit if you want an agency that only serves tax and accounting firms across the country. Hinge may fit a larger, multi-office firm that needs research and a full rebrand.
