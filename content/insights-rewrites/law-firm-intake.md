# Law Firm Intake: A Simple Process for Faster Consultations

**Short answer:** law firm intake is the path from a first call, form or text to a booked consultation. Answer fast, ask only what a conflict check needs, and book the meeting before the person calls the next firm.

| Step | Goal | Keep it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. First reply | Let them know a person heard them | Within minutes, day or night |
| 2. A few safe questions | Route the inquiry | Contact info, practice area, county |
| 3. Conflict check | Clear the firm to talk | Names of the other parties only |
| 4. Book the consultation | Put a time on the calendar | One link or one call |
| 5. Confirm and remind | Get them to show up | A text the day before |

## What is law firm intake?

Intake is everything between "I need a lawyer" and a consultation on the calendar.

It includes:

- Answering the phone, the web form and texts
- Sorting the inquiry by practice area
- Running a conflict check
- Booking, confirming and reminding

Most firms have pieces of it. Few have it written down, timed and covered after 5 PM.

## Why does intake speed matter so much?

People reach out to more than one firm, and the first useful answer usually wins.

In [Clio's 2019 study, as reported by 2Civility](https://www.2civility.org/clio-legal-trends-report-finds-law-firms-arent-responsive-to-potential-clients/):

- 82 percent of consumers ranked timeliness of response as the most important factor when looking for a lawyer
- 64 percent said they had contacted a law firm that never responded
- Only 40 percent of firms answered Clio's test email
- 56 percent of calls were answered by a person, and 39 percent went to voicemail
- More than half of firms didn't return voicemails within 72 hours

The same report found 57 percent of clients searched for a lawyer on their own, not only through a referral. Those people are comparing you in real time.

## What does a simple law firm intake process look like?

### Step 1: Send a first reply fast

Every new inquiry gets an answer within minutes. A missed call gets a text back. A web form gets a reply within a minute.

The reply is short and plain: who you are, that a person will follow up, and a link to book.

### Step 2: Ask a few questions, not the story

Collect only what you need to route the inquiry:

- Name, phone and email
- Practice area (a short dropdown, not a free-text box)
- County or state
- Is anything time-sensitive? Yes or no
- Preferred time for a consultation

Leave out "Tell us what happened." The facts of a matter belong in the consultation, with an attorney, after the conflict check.

### Step 3: Run the conflict check first

Bar rules treat people who consult you as prospective clients. [The Washington State Bar's summary of RPC 1.18](https://wabarnews.org/2025/03/13/rpc-1-18-duties-to-prospective-clients/) notes that what they share is confidential even if they never hire you, and that a website inviting case details without clear warnings can create a consultation.

So intake asks for the names of the other parties, nothing more. [Freeman Mathis & Gary's guidance](https://www.fmglaw.com/professional-liability/just-the-parties-involved-maam-not-the-facts-dont-be-a-joe-friday-when-getting-preliminary-information-from-a-prospective-client/) puts it simply: get the parties, and ask people not to volunteer more than you request.

Your state's rules and your own policies decide the details. This is not legal advice.

### Step 4: Book the consultation on the spot

Don't end a call with "someone will get back to you." Offer two times or send a booking link while they are still on the phone.

### Step 5: Confirm and remind

Send a confirmation right away and a reminder the day before. Include the address or video link and what to bring.

## How should a firm handle intake after hours?

Evenings and weekends are when many people finally sit down to look for a lawyer.

A basic after-hours setup:

- **Missed-call text-back.** The caller gets a text right away instead of a voicemail greeting. See how it works in [our missed-call text-back guide](/insights/missed-call-text-back).
- **A form that answers itself.** An instant reply with a booking link.
- **Online booking.** Real open times on your calendar, so nobody waits for the office to open.

None of this replaces the attorney. It holds the person's place until you can talk.

## What should intake wording avoid?

Intake messages, booking pages and texts are attorney advertising. Keep them clear of:

- **"Specialist" or "expert."** [ABA rules](https://ipethicslaw.com/aba-approves-changes-to-attorney-advertising-ethics-rules/) bar claiming certification unless an approved organization certified you and is named
- **Outcome talk.** No "we'll win," no case values, no past results without the disclaimers your state requires
- **Requests for case facts** by text, chat or form

Every auto-reply should be approved by an attorney before it goes live.

## Which law firm intake metrics should you track?

Five numbers tell you whether intake is working:

| Metric | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Time to first reply | How long a new inquiry waits |
| Calls answered by a person | How often callers reach someone |
| Inquiries to consultations booked | Whether intake gets people to a meeting |
| Consultation show rate | Whether confirmations and reminders work |
| Source of each inquiry | Which channels bring the cases you want |

Review them monthly. Look at after-hours inquiries separately.
