# FindLaw Alternatives for Small Law Firms (2026)

**Short answer:** if you run a 1-10 attorney firm and want a published price, a fast reply to every call and inquiry, and a website you end up owning, compare FindLaw with Justia, Juris Digital, Rankings.io, On The Map Marketing, LawRank and a local option like Axeon. Each fits a different firm.

This guide sticks to what each company says on its own site, checked in October 2026.

Where something isn't published, we say so.

## Who owns FindLaw now?

FindLaw is no longer part of Thomson Reuters.

[Thomson Reuters closed the sale of FindLaw to Internet Brands](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2024/december/thomson-reuters-sells-findlaw-business-to-internet-brands.html) on December 2, 2024.

Internet Brands also operates Nolo, Avvo and Martindale. If you are shopping directory listings, several of the big names now sit under one owner.

## What does FindLaw include?

FindLaw's [services page](https://www.findlaw.com/lawyer-marketing/services/) says it is "Trusted by 17,000+ Firms." It lists:

- Website packages and local SEO
- Legal directory advertising across FindLaw, Super Lawyers, LawInfo and Abogado.com
- Pay-per-click ads, social media and content
- Client intake, ratings and reviews, and INSIGHT analytics

Its [call answering and web chat page](https://www.findlaw.com/lawyer-marketing/services/call-answering-services-and-web-chat/) describes 24/7 website chat and up to 24/7/365 phone coverage.

Operators collect contact details and the issue, then email a summary to the firm.

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026). The intake page says you are "only charged for successful engagements."

## Which companies do firms compare with FindLaw?

### Justia

Justia is known for its lawyer directory. Its website product is Justia Elevate.

- [Elevate sites start at $82.50 monthly when paid annually](https://www.justia.com/marketing/elevate/)
- Built on an enhanced version of WordPress set up for law firms
- Includes tools such as Justia Chat

It is the only company here, other than Axeon, with a published starting price.

### Juris Digital

[Juris Digital](https://jurisdigital.com/) is a law-firm-only agency covering SEO, websites, content, paid search and Local Services Ads.

- Its FAQ says firms sign "a basic agreement committing to a 12-month campaign"
- One-off projects are available
- A product called JurisPage is described as built for firms "early in their growth"

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

### Rankings.io

[Rankings.io](https://rankings.io/) calls personal injury "Our specialty" and mentions working with 300+ PI firms.

- Services include SEO, Google Search Ads, Local Services Ads, website design, paid social and video
- It advertises "Month-to-month flexibility"
- It also lists criminal defense, family law, immigration and other practice areas

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

### On The Map Marketing

[On The Map Marketing](https://www.onthemap.com/) works with law firms and with other industries, including healthcare. Its site lists a Miami, FL address.

Its [law firm SEO pricing guide](https://www.onthemap.com/law-firm-seo-agency/pricing/) says firms can expect to pay "$3,000 to $15,000" monthly to a lawyer SEO agency. That is a general range, not a price list.

**Price:** quote-based; their own pricing is not published on their site (checked October 2026).

### LawRank

[LawRank](https://www.lawrank.com/) says it has spent "more than a decade building rankings exclusively for law firms."

- Services include SEO, AI search optimization, PPC, web design and social media
- Offices listed in San Diego and New York

**Price:** quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

## When may FindLaw or another option be the better fit?

Some firms are better served elsewhere:

- **You want directory exposure.** FindLaw and Justia run large lawyer directories. If listings on those sites matter to you, go to the source.
- **You want live operators answering chats.** FindLaw offers human-staffed chat and call coverage.
- **You are a personal injury firm with a national budget.** Rankings.io is built around PI.
- **You practice in several states.** A national agency with offices in more than one market may suit you.
- **You only want a low-cost website.** Justia Elevate publishes a low starting price.

## What do bar rules mean for your marketing?

Whoever you hire, the ads carry your name.

In Iowa, the [Iowa Rules of Professional Conduct](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/ACO/CourtRulesChapter/32.pdf) cover this in three rules:

- **32:7.1:** no false or misleading statements about you or your services
- **32:7.2:** specific rules, including no "specialist" claim without a named certifying body, and the name and contact information of a responsible lawyer or firm
- **32:7.3:** limits on soliciting clients

Rules 32:7.4 and 32:7.5 are reserved.

Have an attorney approve every page, ad and auto-reply before it goes live. Other states have their own versions. This is not legal advice.
