# LassoMD Alternatives for Dental Practices (2026)

**Short answer:** LassoMD is a solid dental marketing company with published prices and real missed-call software. If you run one or two independent offices in Iowa or the Midwest, Axeon Studio is the alternative to look at: a local team, one published price with the patient follow-up tools included, no markup on ad spend, and a written 90-day exit.

## What is LassoMD?

LassoMD (written Lasso MD) is a [San Diego-based dental marketing and software company](https://www.lassomd.com/company). It was [formerly called Get Practice Growth](https://timiacapital.com/?p=6352).

What it sells, per [its homepage](https://www.lassomd.com/):

- Websites, SEO, Google Ads and social media
- Branding, video and photo
- PatientLoop software that connects with Dentrix, Open Dental and Eaglesoft

A few facts worth knowing:

- It [announced a $2M investment from Trestle Partners](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230427005364/en/) in April 2023.
- [Benco Dental announced](https://www.benco.com/news/lasso-md-and-benco-dental-announce-new-partnership-to-help-more-practices-improve-marketing-results-and-drive-greater-cost-efficiency) in July 2023 that it would resell Lasso's software and services.

## Does LassoMD text back missed calls?

Yes, and that deserves credit. [PatientLoop](https://patientloop.com/dental-practices) texts an unanswered caller within seconds and invites them to book online or text back.

Its [calls page](https://www.lassomd.com/dental-marketing-software/calls-management) also lists AI call grading. PatientLoop's page says "No long contracts."

The catch is how it's priced. On [LassoMD's pricing page](https://www.lassomd.com/pricing):

- Four plans run from $750 to $2,000 monthly
- The PatientLoop software bundle is a $500 monthly add-on
- Online scheduling is listed as a separate add-on
- Google and Facebook ads are listed as "Ad Budget + 25%"
- No setup fee or contract length is listed (checked October 2026)

So the follow-up tools exist. They just sit on top of the plan price, and ad spend carries a percentage.

## Which other dental marketing companies get compared?

These are the names practices most often weigh next to LassoMD. We left out Scorpion, which has [its own comparison](/insights/scorpion-alternatives).

**ProSites.** Based in [Murrieta, CA](https://www.prosites.com/), it says it is trusted by 7,500+ practices and has served dentistry for over 20 years. It lists websites, SEO, ads, an AI receptionist, online booking and a performance dashboard. Its [pricing page](https://www.prosites.com/pricing/) names four plans but shows no monthly prices: quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

**PBHS.** It [started in the late 1970s](https://www.pbhs.com/about-pbhs/) in Santa Rosa, CA, moved into dental websites in the 1990s and has been part of RevenueWell since 2021. It focuses on specialty practices. Quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

**Progressive Dental.** [Founded in 2009](https://www.progressivedentalmarketing.com/), it focuses on implant and full-arch marketing, with a sister brand, The Closing Institute, for case-acceptance training. Quote-based; pricing not published on their site (checked October 2026).

**Gargle.** Based in [Lehi, UT](https://www.gargle.com/), it offers websites, SEO, reviews, social, ads and online scheduling. Its [pricing page](https://www.gargle.com/pricing) lists full-suite plans at $1,495 and $2,095 monthly, and ad management at $599 plus 5 percent of ad spend.

## When is LassoMD or another company the better fit?

Be honest with yourself about what you need.

- **You run a DSO or many locations across states.** ProSites and PBHS have the scale and long track records for that.
- **You are a specialist.** PBHS is built around specialty practices.
- **You mainly want implant and full-arch cases.** Progressive Dental is built for exactly that, including sales training.
- **You want heavy video production and deep PM-system links.** LassoMD leans on video and connects to Dentrix, Open Dental and Eaglesoft.
- **You want to pick services one at a time.** Gargle prices each piece separately.
- **You're outside Iowa and want a team in your own region.** A local team is only an edge if it's local to you.

## What should you ask any dental marketing company?

Take this list to every call, including ours:

- What happens when a patient calls at 7 PM and nobody answers?
- Is missed-call follow-up in the base price or an add-on?
- Do you take a percentage of my ad spend?
- What is the setup fee and the contract length, in writing?
- Who owns the website and the code if I leave?
